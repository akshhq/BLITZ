BLITZ / ABOUT

Silicon Minds, Circuited Hearts.

BLITZ is the departmental society of the Department of Computer Science at Keshav Mahavidyalaya, University of Delhi. Through workshops, seminars, hackathons, technical competitions, and collaborative initiatives, BLITZ empowers students to master computational thinking, build real-world software, and explore modern technology beyond the classroom.

WHAT WE DO

01

WORKSHOPS & SEMINARS

Hands-on interactive laboratory sessions exploring systems architecture, modern languages, and emergent technologies.

02

TECHNICAL EVENTS

Intense build sprints, speed coding, algorithmic challenges, and bug-hunts promoting sharp logical problem solving.

03

PROJECTS & INITIATIVES

Practical engineering where students construct open source tooling, experiments, and production-grade prototypes.

04

COMMUNITY & COLLABORATION

An inclusive peer-learning space connecting undergraduate developers, alumni mentors, and industry practitioners.
